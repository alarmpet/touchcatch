# Simulation model

Every report pins seed, report version, ruleset version and `botModelVersion`. Fifty matches are PR wiring smoke; 10,000 are nightly bot sensitivity evidence; 100,000 draws are economy-model evidence. All are `DRAFT_TEST_ONLY` unless a separately approved decision says otherwise. Bot outputs cannot stand in for human satisfaction, learning, fairness or a powered A/B experiment.

Balance candidates preserve mean match duration 50–70 seconds, final-rush entry 40–65%, final challenge attempt at least 70%, and wrong final-answer submissions 0.5–1.5 per eligible unlocked match. These are observational candidates, not G2 pass/fail gates without real telemetry.
